# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Solo intermediate lifter using the app between sets in a gym. Phone in one hand, barbell in the other. Needs to log weight and reps in under 3 seconds, glance at what's next, and move on. No browsing, no exploring; pure task execution under fatigue.

## Product Purpose

A personal training companion for the Lvysaur Intermediate Aesthetic Routine. Tracks a fixed 5-day/week, 3-week periodized program with automatic set tapering, PR progression, and deload cycles. Syncs to Supabase for cloud backup and cross-device access. The app replaces a notebook: it knows the program, remembers your weights, and tells you exactly what to do next.

## Brand Personality

Raw, powerful, precise. The interface should feel like a forged instrument: dark, dense, purposeful. No decoration that doesn't serve the lift. Every element earns its screen space.

## Anti-references

Generic fitness apps: no bright gradients, no gamification badges, no social feeds, no motivational quotes, no progress confetti, no "streak" mechanics. Not MyFitnessPal, not Fitbod, not Strong's cheerful cards. This is a tool, not a coach.

## Design Principles

1. **One glance, one action.** Every screen answers one question: "What do I do next?" Minimize reading, maximize doing.
2. **Respect the context.** The user is tired, sweating, holding a phone between sets. Large touch targets, high contrast, zero scroll to reach the primary action.
3. **Density over decoration.** Show more useful data, less chrome. Whitespace is earned by information hierarchy, not applied uniformly.
4. **The program is the authority.** The app doesn't ask what you want to do; it tells you. Opinionated defaults, minimal configuration.
5. **Disappear when not needed.** Rest timers, warmup suggestions, and PR tracking surface only when relevant. No persistent UI for dormant features.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast ratios. Large touch targets (minimum 44pt) for gym use with sweaty hands. Readable at arm's length. One-handed operation as primary interaction mode.
